## GBM Motivation

As Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) become more common in many industries, these advanced technologies are also used in the insurance sector to enhance processes. One powerful ML technique is Gradient Boosting Machines (GBM). 

In previous lessons, we explored Generalized Linear Models (GLMs) and their use in predicting insurance outcomes based on past data trends. While GLMs are easy to understand and explain, they might miss some complex patterns in the data. This is where GBM can help.

There are many use cases for GBM in insurance. For example, we use it to predict claim frequency, the amount of losses, burning costs, and risks per unit of exposure. Generally, We use GBM to improve GLM. GBM can be used alongside GLM to enhance the modeling process. By first running a GBM, we can explore model and variable trends, uncover hidden patterns, and identify the most important variables. This information can then be used to create GLMs more efficiently and improve our GLMs, making our predictions more accurate and reliable.

**Reasons to Use GBM**

1. **Better Predictions**: GBM is a powerful ensemble learning method that combines multiple simple models to create a stronger, more accurate model. This results in improved accuracy and predictive power compared to traditional linear models.

2. **Finding Complex Patterns**: Insurance data often contains complicated interactions and non-linear patterns that may be challenging to identify using GLMs alone. GBM excels in capturing these complexities, providing a more comprehensive understanding of the underlying data.

3. **Important Factors and Interactions**: One of the key advantages of GBM is its ability to identify the most important variables and their interactions within the dataset. By running a GBM model, we can gain insights into which factors are most important for our predictions and how these factors interact with each other.

4. **Staying Up-to-Date**: Using the latest AI and ML techniques like GBM keeps us competitive. It ensures our predictions are as accurate and reliable as possible.

In summary, GBM helps us find and use complex patterns in our data. By combining GBM with GLM, we can make better predictions and make smarter decisions for our insurance portfolios.

# Image prompts for the Lesson 6 quiz (Kahoot / Cahoot background images)

Prompts for an image model (used: `gemini-3-pro-image-preview`) for the background pictures of the 12 quiz questions in
`Lessons/Lesson6_alt/Support/quiz_L6.md` (saved in `Lessons/Lesson6_alt/Support/quiz_images/img_Q*.png`).
`generate_images.py` reads this file with `--prompts` (see `README.md`), so keep the structure: each prompt is a
```` ```text ```` block under a `###` heading that ends with the output filename in backticks.

The pictures only set the topic of the question - none of them shows or hints at the correct answer. They contain no
text at all, because the quiz tool overlays the question and the answers.

## Common style (prepended to every prompt)

```text
Flat vector illustration used as a quiz background, landscape 16:9. Simple composition, the main subject is centred and
not too small, with calm empty space along the top and bottom edges. Do not draw buttons, banners, title bars, cards
or any user-interface elements.
Deep indigo-blue background (#1E2A5A) with a soft lighter blue glow in the centre; the objects use a limited palette of
white, warm yellow (#FFC83D), teal (#2EC4B6) and coral (#FF6B6B). No gradients on the objects, no 3D, no shadows, no
photorealism. Friendly and slightly humorous tone.
ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT: no letters, no digits, no words, no numbers, no currency or other symbols that are characters,
no labels, no axis ticks, no captions, no logos, no watermark. Coins are plain discs with a simple ring, nothing on them. No human faces; if a person is needed, draw a faceless simple figure.
```

